🛡️ Deleted Chat & Safety Moderation Policy

Anti-Bullying Audit Archive • Compliance with IT Intermediary Rules, 2021 & Digital Safety

1. Immediate Client-Side Deletion

When a user selects "Delete for Everyone" or "Delete for Me", the message is immediately removed from the active conversation view on all devices. No deleted messages remain visible in student chat screens.

2. Why Deleted Messages are Audited

In online student environments, offenders sometimes send hurtful, threatening, or vulgar content and quickly tap "Delete for Everyone" to erase evidence before a victim can capture proof. To protect victims, Chatnya maintains a cryptographic audit snapshot in a secure administrative archive (deletedMessages).

3. Restricted Administrative Access Only

Deleted chat audit records are STRICTLY RESTRICTED to authorized platform administrators and discipline coordinators. No peer or regular user can access or view deleted message archives.

4. Legal & Child Protection Compliance

This policy complies with Rule 3(1)(h) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, which requires platforms to preserve records for investigating cyber offenses.

5. Retention Schedule & Permanent Purging

Deleted message audit logs follow a rolling 30-to-90 day security retention window. Routine deleted records not linked to an active safety report or bullying complaint are permanently purged. Account deletion permanently eradicates all user chats after 7 days.

6. Appeals & Grievance Redressal

Any user subject to disciplinary action has the right to an administrative review. For appeals or inquiries, contact: chatnya@krishna-devlopments.site.
